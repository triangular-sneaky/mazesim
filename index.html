mazesim — controls

Grid map (top-down)

Click a cell to select. N is up, W is left.

Panel — none

Each tile: left bar = h (E-W, amber), right bar = v (N-S, blue) — independent. Drag up/down to set height; alt/right-click to blink.

Movements

—
Cruise speed for all movement (constant, with slight ease in/out).

Video overlay

—
scroll = zoom, drag = pan, dbl-click = reset
Composites the maze over a live camera feed — solid walls/floor become a wireframe cage. Grant camera access when prompted, or pick Video file… to overlay onto an uploaded clip instead. Adjust fov (vertical field of view) to match the source's perspective. Tick adjust to zoom/pan the video with the mouse (the maze view is locked while it's on).
